How to use this course

Every session follows the same order. Work through it from the top, then tick the checklist at the end. Your ticks and answers are saved.

Until Rhino is installed you can still do the reading, the live examples and the Predict and Fix the bug exercises in every session. Open a build challenge's solution only after you have tried it. Japanese terms you will hear at a design office are given in brackets after the English, and collected in the glossary.

The last stage, Projects, works like a small office job. Each project has a brief, a list of what to hand in, and a comparison table (比較検討表) that ranks your options from the numbers you get in Rhino.
